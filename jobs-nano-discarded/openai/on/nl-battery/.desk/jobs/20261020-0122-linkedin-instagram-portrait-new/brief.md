## Deliverable
Social card announcing Northlight’s new battery option, adapted for LinkedIn and Instagram portrait. One self-contained responsive HTML file, with one artboard and the same DOM at both sizes.

## Sizes
`linkedin`: 1200×627, 6% safe zone on all sides.
`ig-post`: 1080×1350, 6% safe zone at the sides and top, 10% at the bottom.

## Copy
Seven years to break even. Then free.
Battery now included.
northlight.energy

## Brand
`.desk/DESIGN.md`. Warm paper background (#f7f5f0), near-black text (#161614), and one orange accent (#d9480f) used once.
Left-aligned type with generous margins. Voice is warm, plain and numerate; lead with the seven-year claim.

## Fonts
Inter, Arial, sans-serif; headline weight 600, body weight 400. Bundled availability is not supplied; confirm Inter before building and inline the font.
Use one family, two weights and at most three sizes. Headline at least 44px landscape and 65px portrait.

## Must be true
Preserve all supplied copy verbatim; add no claims, qualifiers or CTA. Keep the headline within three lines landscape and four portrait, in the top 40% on portrait; avoid an empty band exceeding one-third of the canvas.
Use only the brand palette; no sky gradients, sun icons, leaves, stock illustrations or invented logo. Meet the social-card standard’s contrast, spacing and thumbnail readability requirements.
Inline all assets; no network, animation or image-based text. One `<main data-artboard>` fills the viewport and adapts with media queries.

## Scope
Quick: one social-card design in two sizes, with brand and copy supplied. Three roughs using editorial split, type only and frame; Art Director pick, builder polish, mechanical proof and Creative Director sign-off. Studio quality; maximum six rounds.
