## Deliverable
Social card about home solar panels still paying their way in January, adapted for LinkedIn and Instagram portrait. One self-contained, responsive HTML file with real DOM text and inline assets.

## Sizes
`linkedin` — 1200×627; `ig-post` — 1080×1350. Keep 6% outer margins, increasing the portrait bottom margin to 10%.

## Copy
January still pays.
Snow slides off. The numbers don't.
northlight.energy

## Brand
`.desk/DESIGN.md` — Northlight. Warm paper `#f7f5f0`, dark text `#161614`, and one orange `#d9480f` accent. Left-aligned typography, generous margins, and a warm, plain, numerate voice.

## Fonts
Inter, with headline weight 600 and body weight 400; Arial, sans-serif fallback. Bundled availability is unverified; confirm before building and inline the selected font consistently across both sizes.

## Must be true
Preserve all supplied copy verbatim; use the URL as a small brand mark. No invented output figures, savings, or guarantees; no sky gradients, sun icons, leaves, stock illustrations, or additional accent colours.
Headline must read at thumbnail size and occupy the portrait’s top 40%; avoid an empty middle band taller than one-third of the canvas. Use three type sizes maximum, accessible contrast, and no network assets or animation.

## Scope
Quick: one social-card design in two sizes, with brand and copy supplied. Three rough directions, Art Director pick, builder polish, mechanical proof, and Creative Director sign-off; studio quality, capped at six rounds.
