## Deliverable
Social card about winter solar output: panels still paying their way in January. One self-contained HTML file adapting to LinkedIn landscape and Instagram portrait.

## Sizes
`linkedin` — 1200×627, 6% safe margin on all sides.
`ig-post` — 1080×1350, 6% safe margin at sides and top, 10% at bottom.

## Copy
January still pays.
Snow slides off. The numbers don't.
northlight.energy

## Brand
Use `.desk/DESIGN.md`: warm paper `#f7f5f0`, dark text `#161614`, and one orange accent `#d9480f`.
Left-align with generous margins; keep the voice warm, plain and numerate. No sky gradients, sun icons or leaves.

## Fonts
Inter, Arial, sans-serif; headline 600, body 400. Bundled font availability is unverified in the supplied context.
Inline Inter if available; otherwise use Arial consistently. Keep three text sizes, headline line height 1.0–1.1, and readable text at least 24px.

## Must be true
Preserve all supplied copy verbatim, with the headline dominant and the domain a small corner mark. Do not invent output figures, savings, guarantees or additional claims.
Use one DOM with `<main data-artboard>`, responsive composition, inline assets, no network or animation. Portrait headline stays in the top 40%; avoid an empty band exceeding one third of the canvas.
Explore editorial split, type only and frame directions. Meet the social-card standard for contrast, margins and thumbnail readability; use at most one supporting visual.

## Scope
Quick: one concept adapted to two sizes, existing brand and supplied copy. Three roughs, Art Director pick, builder polish and Creative Director sign-off.
Studio quality; six-round cap. Runner handles staffing, rendering, proof and state.
