## 2 Method

### 2.1 The pipeline

Desk Studio's runner, `desk-run`, plays a design job as a fixed loop of single-turn model calls, one per desk, each desk working from its own written brief at a director, senior or builder tier. An intake call at the director tier writes a brief with the sizes and the copy that must appear word for word. Three roughs follow in parallel at the senior tier, one for each of the first three composition patterns in the social card standard. The Art Director picks one from the screenshots and writes at most five notes. Rounds then begin. A round is one polish call by the builder, a render, the proof step, and, if proof passes, a sign-off by the Creative Director. A failed proof sends its failures to the builder as the next round's notes and the Creative Director never sees that round, so it costs a round and a builder call but no director call. <!-- source: C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/runner/run.mjs (stepIntake, stepRoughs, stepPick, stepPolish, stepProof, stepSignoff), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/skills/desk/roles/art-director.md (five notes at most), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/skills/desk/standards/social-card.md -->

The loop counts consecutive misses per failing key, a proof check or a rubric axis under 8. The third consecutive miss of one key sends the next polish round to the senior model, and three more send it to the Art Director, who rewrites the file for one round. A cap on rounds across polish, proof and sign-off ends the job under the bar if reached. I set the cap to 6 for every job, though the runner's default is 8. By my reading, that lets a job reach the senior model at round 4 at the earliest and never the Art Director, whose first possible round is 7, which matches the runner's own escalation test. <!-- source: C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/runner/run.mjs (afterMiss, runJob), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/runner/test/loop.test.mjs (escalation test), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio-research/proof-ablation-2026-10-20/exp-run.mjs (--rounds 6), rounds 4 and 7 derived from the miss counter -->

### 2.2 The proof step

Proof runs after every polish round and never calls a model. It opens the file in headless Chrome at each named size at device scale 1, takes a screenshot and a second one with all text made transparent, so the pixels behind the text can be measured, and records every text run. Runs that are hidden, below 0.9 effective opacity or inside a container clipped to one pixel are left out of the text the checks examine. A round passes only if no size has a failure.

| Check | Fails when |
| --- | --- |
| font | the brand's first family is not loaded in the file |
| overflow | the document is over 1 px wider or taller than the canvas, or a text box sits off the canvas or outside a clipping ancestor by over 0.5 px |
| safe zone | a text box crosses 6% of the canvas on any side, or 10% at the bottom of ig-post (135 px) |
| contrast | the worst contrast against the text-hidden render, sampled every 2 px under the box, falls under 4.5:1, or under 3:1 for text at 24 px and up or at 18.67 px and up with weight 700 or more |
| type scale | a size is under 20 px here, or ln(size/base)/ln(ratio) is over 0.12 from a whole number |
| palette | more than 5% of pixels, sampled every 4th both ways, are over 45 RGB units from every brand colour |
| copy | a required string is absent from the rendered text, compared lowercase without whitespace or quote marks, or the page has no readable text |
| size | an email file is over 102 KB, which never applies to these cases |

The sizes are linkedin at 1200x627 and ig-post at 1080x1350. The documentation and the builder prompt call the type scale tolerance 12 percent, though the code measures 0.12 of a scale step, a size error of about 2.7 percent at ratio 1.25. <!-- source: C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/proof/check.mjs (runChecks and each check), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/proof/cli.mjs (102 KB), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/proof/browser.mjs (0.9 opacity, one pixel clip), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/proof/sizes.mjs (sizes, safe fractions), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/skills/desk/procedures/proof.md (12% wording), 135 px and 2.7 percent derived from the fractions, base and ratio -->

### 2.3 The sign-off rubric

The Creative Director scores eight axes from 0 to 10 on the screenshots and never on code: hierarchy, typography, spacing and rhythm, colour, composition, originality, brand fit and copy. A job reaches the bar when every axis is 8 or above and the average is 8.5 or above. The runner recomputes the average and the verdict from the eight scores, so the director's own pass line does not decide. The brief says "Be hard on originality and composition" and asks for an instruction with numbers in it on every axis under 9. The sign-off prompt carries the brief, brand, standard, house style, rubric, taste file, the Art Director's pick reason and notes, and one proof line. It carries no earlier scores, so each sign-off judges the current screenshots fresh. <!-- source: C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/skills/desk/rubric.md, C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/skills/desk/roles/creative-director.md, C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/runner/parse.mjs (parseScores), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/runner/run.mjs (stepSignoff) -->

### 2.4 The ablation

`--no-proof` is a flag I added to the runner on a branch of the public repository. With it the proof step still renders the file at the same sizes in the same browser, records a skipped entry and goes straight to sign-off, so no check runs and no failure becomes a note. The one prompt change is a line in the sign-off call. With proof on it reads "Proof passed this round (overflow, safe zone, contrast on real pixels, type scale, palette, copy present)." With proof off it reads "No mechanical proof ran this round."

Everything else is shared, including the models, briefs, rubric, standard, brand file, ladder and cap. That includes two texts that still mention proof. Every prompt carrying the brand file, the directors' included, lists the brand scale as "the only sizes proof accepts", and the runner note tells each desk to skip any proof its brief mentions because the runner runs it after the desk returns. I left both so the arms differ in the check alone, which means an off-arm builder is told the rules and never told whether it broke them. In the off arm every round reaches a sign-off. <!-- source: C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/runner/run.mjs (stepProof proofMode off, stepSignoff, scaleLine, RUNNER_NOTE), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio-research/proof-ablation-2026-10-20/README.md -->

### 2.7 Measures and the hidden audit

Each job's `state.json` gives reached the bar (the last sign-off passed and the job was not handed back under the bar), rounds (which counts proof-failed rounds), cost by tier, every sign-off's axis scores and notes, and the escalations in its desk list. Three jobs, the resumed ones, have a cost total with some token counts unknown and are flagged; no job failed after its resume. The state file keeps the number of proof failures per round, and the run log names the failing checks without counting them.

Every Creative Director note was coded by what it asks the builder to change, into six mechanical categories that mirror the proof checks (safe zone, overflow, contrast, copy, type scale, palette), eight taste categories (hierarchy, composition, spacing, typography, colour, originality, brand, copy) and other. Two readers coded every note independently, each a single call to claude-sonnet-5-5 with a different brief, one as a design director and one as a QA engineer who writes rendering checks, in batches of 25 notes with the category definitions and the rule that a note naming a canvas edge, margin or safe zone is mechanical even when it also talks about balance, and that a size asked for so the text reads at the platform size is mechanical while a size asked for emphasis is taste. A third reader with the same definitions settled every disagreement, seeing both codes and reasons. I report agreement between the two readers at the category level and at the mechanical-or-taste level, and the earlier regular-expression coding stays in the analysis output beside it so the two can be compared.

I also re-ran the checks offline on every saved round render of every job, on arm and off, from the DOM and pixel captures the runner keeps, which gives the mechanical fault count at each round whether or not proof ran. On the on arm this reproduces the proof results exactly.

The hidden audit runs once per finished job. The proof command checks the signed-off `final.html` at the job's sizes against the brand file with the case's own three copy lines, taken from the case file and not from the intake's reading of the ask, and writes `audit.json`. No desk sees it. With proof on it repeats a check the last round passed, so it passes by construction unless the job ended at the cap or the intake altered a copy line. With proof off its failures by check count the mechanical faults that reached sign-off. <!-- source: C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio-research/proof-ablation-2026-10-20/README.md, C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio/runner/run.mjs, C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio-research/proof-ablation-2026-10-20/exp-run.mjs (audit), C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio-research/proof-ablation-2026-10-20/jobs/anthropic/on/nl-winter/run.log -->

### 2.8 Analysis

I wrote four hypotheses down before any job finished. With proof off, signed-off work carries mechanical faults the director passed (H1). With proof on, jobs use more rounds and cost more (H2). The director's scores do not fall when proof is off (H3). The notes differ in kind between arms (H4). Within each family I pair jobs by case, test rounds, cost and the final rubric average with an exact two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test, and test reaching the bar with an exact McNemar test. Each paired mean difference carries a seeded percentile bootstrap 95% interval, dz and the matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation. Audit failures are counted by check, size and brand. H1 is read on the signed-off files, the jobs that reached the bar, since a job that ends at the cap hands back its last attempt under the bar with the proof report attached rather than a sign-off; the all-jobs figures are reported beside them. Each family gets a table and a pooled table follows, with 15 pairs per family and 30 pooled. The pooled tests are secondary, since the two families differ in model and price. The per-axis comparisons and the first sign-off average are exploratory and unadjusted. I make no claim beyond what 15 pairs carry. <!-- source: C:/Users/adity/Desktop/Adi/deskstudio-research/proof-ablation-2026-10-20/README.md (hypotheses, analysis plan), 15 and 30 pairs derived from cases.json -->
